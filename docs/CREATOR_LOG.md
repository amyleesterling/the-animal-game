This records what actually happened. Sophia's ideas and decisions belong to
Sophia; implementation choices made by adults or agents are labeled separately.

## Original direction — recorded in the project plan

Sophia imagined an animal-world exploration game. The existing plan records
the goals: living habitats, short questions, wildlife photographs, a personal
field book, and eventual travel to other worlds. Amy, Cora, and AI support
research, making, and revision. The first selected encounter was a plains zebra.

## 2026-09-19 — First playable zebra expedition

Amy asked for a v1 and an agent fleet using gstack. A Codex coordinator assigned
three parallel agents to the world, content/saves, and setup/independent QA.
The coordinator built the interface and integrated the work. The team used
gstack's supported instruction-only digest and documented review stages.

Implementation decisions made by the team:

- Build the plan's complete zebra slice before adding all ten animals.
- Use a warm, stylized procedural landscape and zebra as original placeholder art.
- Keep discoveries and photographs local to the browser.
- Provide a “Guide me” route so a child can finish without controlling a 3D camera.
- Offer another try or a gentle continuation after an incorrect answer.

Testing caught mobile photograph cropping and a reset/save timing bug. The
team corrected both and retained regression coverage. These are engineering
observations, not evidence of child playtesting or wildlife-expert approval.
