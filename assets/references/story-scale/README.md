**Do not infer real size from pixels.** Each image was framed independently. For a truthful scale bar, a sourced measurement with a defined endpoint and a separately calibrated display scale are needed. The sex-specific research table is in [`docs/research/ANIMAL_ADULT_SIZE_TABLE.md`](../../../docs/research/ANIMAL_ADULT_SIZE_TABLE.md). That review found no reliable male/female *ranges* for ground-to-top-of-head height for these seven species; it keeps shoulder height and overall height explicitly distinct.

The story calls its giraffe a northern giraffe, but the Tarangire–Manyara ecosystem is associated with Masai giraffe. That species mismatch needs a product decision before the art or scale value is treated as park-specific.

## Generation prompt set

All seven used the `scientific-educational` use case and this common direction: “photorealistic adult animal cutout for a children's safari height-comparison notebook; exact 90-degree left-facing side profile; calm square standing stance, all four feet on one invisible horizontal baseline; entire animal including ears, horns where present, tail and feet visible with clear margins; true transparent alpha; no floor, shadow, scenery, text, labels, rulers or other animals; even natural light; visual reference only, not a calibrated measurement.” The distinct animal/anatomy instructions were:

- **Plains zebra:** *Equus quagga*, credible natural fur and stripe pattern, mane and all four hooves visible.
- **African savanna elephant:** *Loxodonta africana*, adult female with modest tusks, natural hanging trunk, large African elephant ears and realistic gray wrinkled skin.
- **Northern giraffe:** *Giraffa camelopardalis*, upright neck and head, natural irregular brown patches separated by pale lines, all ossicones visible.
- **Common warthog:** *Phacochoerus africanus*, adult with modest curved tusks, rough gray-brown hide, snout, ears and mane visible.
- **Thomson's gazelle:** *Eudorcas thomsonii*, tan-and-white coat, bold black flank stripe, white rump, slender body and ringed curved horns.
- **Cheetah:** *Acinonyx jubatus*, slender athletic body, spotted golden coat, dark facial tear lines and complete ringed tail; no running pose.
- **Spotted hyena:** *Crocuta crocuta*, rounded ears, tawny spotted coat, strong jaws, shoulder-high sloping back, short bushy tail; no stripes.
