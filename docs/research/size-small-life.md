# Adult size evidence: small savanna life

Reviewed 21 September 2026 for the nine profiles in `src/content/savanna-small-life.ts`. These are *species-specific source measurements*, not scale values inferred from rendered images. “Not established” means the cited sources reviewed here did not give that measurement; it is **not** a zero or a claim that no such study exists. A mean ± standard deviation/error is **not** a biological range. All masses below are live/fresh body mass unless labelled dry.

For the proposed photo scale bar, standing height would mean level ground to the top of the head **in a specified pose**, excluding quills, wings and antennae. Neither horizontal body length nor wingspan can be substituted for it. A butterfly/moth has no stable upright standing pose comparable with a quadruped; termites also vary drastically by caste. Keep the height bar disabled for these profiles until an appropriate measured head-height range and the displayed model’s matching posture are documented.

## Implementation implications

- Preserve `null`/“not established” for unavailable sex-specific **ranges**. Never turn `mean ± SD/SE` into a supposed min–max range or silently assign pooled numbers to both sexes.
- Insect panels can teach **body length** or **wingspan** with a separate visual ruler, but a vertical ground-to-head “height” bar implies a biological standard that these sources do not supply. For termites, the model must state caste (the current small-life research note says it should be a wingless soldier).
- For the three rodents, seek a standardized live animal head-height study or a measured, posture-controlled side profile before adding a truthful vertical height bar. Springhare standing upright, porcupine erected quills, and mole-rat tunnel posture would yield very different numbers.
- Several of these species are not typical visible Tarangire surface fauna; retain the broader African-savanna framing already documented in `SAVANNA_SMALL_LIFE.md`.

## Source checks and limits

The porcupine row uses a published species account that explicitly reproduces sexed Zimbabwe field ranges. The springhare sexed sample is a research paper with **means**, so it cannot fill the requested range cells. For grass mouse, the zoo offers a pooled interval only. For desert locust, the paper's sample adult status and sexed means are explicit, but no observed min/max are printed. For dung beetle, termite and moth, no defensible sex-specific live mass ranges appeared in the reviewed species-specific studies or institutions. The dung-beetle dry mass is placed only in the alternative column because water content makes it unsuitable as an adult live weight.
