# Adult size evidence for all 32 safari species

Research reviewed 21 September 2026. This is Claude's data-entry table for the 7 story animals and 25 expansion animals. It records **adult male and female ranges when the cited source actually gives them**. `—` means a defensible sex-specific range for that measurement was not established in this review; it is not zero. All weights are live body mass unless a row says otherwise. Ranges from different populations and sources are not stitched together into invented species-wide limits.

The [CSV version](animal-adult-size-ranges.csv) has one row per species and numeric min/max columns for direct import. Empty CSV cells mean `not established`, never zero. Only 12 species have supported sex-specific weight intervals in this review; no species has the requested sex-specific head-height interval.

**Height definition requested for the field book:** level ground to the **top of the head**, in a specified standing pose. None of the reviewed sources gives separate male and female *ranges* with that exact endpoint for these 32 species. Every head-height cell therefore remains `—`. Published **shoulder height**, loosely defined overall bird height, body length, and wingspan are listed separately where useful; none should silently populate a head-height field. For moths, butterflies and some other small or posture-changing species, a fixed standing-height bar may not be biologically meaningful. The generated PNGs in [`assets/references/story-scale`](../../assets/references/story-scale/README.md) are uncalibrated art.

All range cells below are in **kg** for weight and would be in **cm** for head height. Point estimates and means remain in the “Other evidence” column, never in a range cell. Each row links to its direct supporting source; the [story](size-story.md), [small-life](size-small-life.md), and [other expansion](size-expansion.md) reviews explain sample, sex, taxonomy, and endpoint limits in detail.

## Field-book implementation rule

Store each measurement with its **kind, endpoint, units, sex scope, life stage, population/region, statistic type, and source URL**. A range is different from a mean, an upper bound, and an observed sample span. For the proposed height bar, either (1) label and position a **shoulder-height** bar for species with suitable sourced data, (2) use a broad, sex-pooled *overall-height* guide where the endpoint is defined well enough, or (3) leave the numeric scale off and let the child estimate an observation. Do not use image pixel height as the biological value.

The current story's [Tarangire-inspired arrival](../STORY_SAFARI.md) and the northern giraffe label conflict with the [Giraffe Conservation Foundation's Tanzania distribution](https://giraffeconservation.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/GCF-Tanzanias-Giraffe-Poster-2022-LR.pdf). Prefer a Masai giraffe for that setting, with its own rechecked measurements and image, before final release.
