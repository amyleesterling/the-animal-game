## What is implemented

The TypeScript/Vite/Three.js game has a separate original zebra slice (`src/main.ts`) and a larger safari (`src/safari.ts`). The safari has a seven-stop story plus 25 additional researched species, a Tarangire-inspired **fictional** approach and compact savanna, Sophia and Cora walking together, a drivable vintage Land Cruiser with rotating wheels, animal proximity encounters, in-game photo composition, a searchable field book, and IndexedDB progress. Models, photographs, and research references are recorded in `public/assets-manifest.json`, `public/models/CREDITS.md`, and `docs/research/`. The game does not contain a geospatially accurate 3D Tanzania or Earth.

The latest Codex change makes the encounter sequence: **“Animal ahead!” → child’s best-guess name (or reveal) → actual species name → estimated height, visible colors, number seen → quiz → framed photo → field book.** Guesses are saved even when misspelled; estimates are observations, not graded answers. Sophia and Cora remain visible and the scene can be dragged during notes and questions, while travel is paused. The captured photo opens the field book and gets a taped Polaroid treatment with the child’s notes. State transitions and versioned save validation are in `src/state/safari.ts`; world input and visibility are in `src/game/safari-world.ts`; the current Polaroid decorator is `src/ui/field-photo.ts` and `.css`.

Amy explicitly asked to stop the robotic narrator while keeping her music alone. Both game paths now **never speak automatically**. Only an explicit “Read it aloud” click invokes browser speech; `src/accessibility/narration.ts` cancels speech when the tab loses focus. The obsolete automatic-narration checkbox was removed. `playwright.config.ts` launches test Chrome with `--mute-audio` so test runs do not interrupt other audio. Do not reintroduce `speechSynthesis.speak()` on render, route transitions, proximity, or page load.
