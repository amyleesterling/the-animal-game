# Adult size evidence: 16 additional savanna animals

Researched 2026-09-21 for the field-notebook height scale. Sex-specific mass ranges below are adult figures where the source states that scope; pooled guide figures may not specify age. All units are metric. `—` means I did not find a defensible value, not zero. **Do not use length, shoulder height, or wingspan as ground-to-top-of-head standing height.** Sources rarely report a separate male and female standing-height *range*. A shared (sex-pooled) range must remain labelled as such in the interface. A reported "height" from a zoo is a useful broad species reference, but the source usually does not specify whether head feathers, casque, or horn tips are included. Species without a documented endpoint should be shown without a numerical head-height scale until a measured specimen or validated physically scaled 3D model is available; an image alone cannot supply that dimension.

## Implementation implications

1. Preserve the source's measurement type in data (`standingHeight`, `shoulderHeight`, `bodyLength`, `totalLength`, `mass`, `meanMass`) and sex scope (`male`, `female`, `pooled`). Do not convert one type to another from image pixels.
2. A sex-pooled standing-height range can be presented as a broad adult range for secretarybird, southern ground hornbill, helmeted guineafowl, grey crowned crane and white-backed vulture. The marabou source supplies only an upper bound. None of these sources supports **separate** male and female standing-height ranges.
3. For meerkat, oxpecker, roller, Nile monitor and other posture-sensitive species, let the student record an observation or compare with a known object, but leave the authoritative numerical head-top scale unavailable until a matched side-on measurement is documented.
4. For adult sex-specific mass, the useful range data here are ground hornbill, aardvark, meerkat, vervet monkey, and African buffalo. Olive baboon has sex-specific *means* rather than ranges. The rest need better data; don't copy a pooled range into separate male/female fields.

## Follow-up: one pooled adult range per species

This is the concise import view. Heights mean **feet on level ground to the top of the resting head** as far as a source's “height” wording permits; bird sources generally do not publish a measurement protocol, so confirm the head/casque/crown endpoint before precise bar calibration. `Not established` is intentional. The three height facts already in the bird profiles—secretarybird, southern ground hornbill, and grey crowned crane—remain represented here. Weight is live body mass, not an image-derived estimate. A pooled range covers both sexes together; it does not imply that each sex spans the entire range.
