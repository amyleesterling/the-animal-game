# Adult standing height, full length, and weight: 32 safari species

Reviewed 21 September 2026. This is the **current field-notebook handoff**. Supporting sex-specific and measurement-endpoint evidence is preserved in [story](size-story.md), [small-life](size-small-life.md), and [other expansion](size-expansion.md) notes; the old [sex-specific CSV](animal-adult-size-ranges.csv) is research, not an import file for the height bar.

**Standing height** is level ground to the top of the adult head at rest, in metres—not shoulder height, body length, wingspan, horn/ear tips, or rendered asset size. ‘Not established’ is a missing value, never zero. Six bird sources publish overall/standing height but do not define the crown, crest, or casque endpoint precisely. [The AZA giraffe manual](https://assets.speakcdn.com/assets/2332/aza_giraffe_care_manual.pdf) explicitly gives top-of-head *means* (male 5.5 m, female 4.3 m), while [SeaWorld](https://seaworld.org/animals/facts/mammals/giraffe/) gives 4.25–5.50 m to top of head; neither is a **northern-giraffe-specific adult range**, so the northern row remains unfilled.

**Full length** includes the tail where one exists. Mammal entries use nose to tail tip when a source actually reports *total length*; bird entries use reported bill-to-tail length; insects use head-to-abdomen body length and have no tail. Where only head–body and tail lengths are reported separately, both are shown but **not added into a purported observed range**. Some source-defined ‘length’ endpoints remain approximate; see linked research notes. Numbers from mixed-age or two-specimen samples are labeled and must not be imported as adult species ranges.

**Adult weight** is a source-published adult or typical-species live-body-mass range in kilograms. A pooled outer envelope combines documented female and male ranges where available. It is not a universal biological limit. **†** means the institution's species-size guide does not explicitly say that every weight in its interval came from an adult sample; treat it as a broad guide. “H” and “W” in the Sources column mark height and weight evidence; each length source is linked in its own cell.

## Notebook use

Only six bird rows have a published overall/standing-height interval. None of the mammals, rodents, insects or reptile has a verified ground-to-top-of-head **range** in these sources. The bird values can guide broad scale, but the crown/casque/crest endpoint needs checking against the posed artwork before a precise photo bar is shown. For other animals, let the student record an estimate without presenting an authoritative answer. Preserve “not established” as blank/null, never `0`.

The game's **northern giraffe** label conflicts with its Tarangire-inspired setting: [Giraffe Conservation Foundation identifies Masai giraffe there](https://giraffeconservation.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/GCF-Tanzanias-Giraffe-Poster-2022-LR.pdf). The Rothschild/Nubian weight above is only a northern-form reference; reassess it if the encounter changes to Masai giraffe.
