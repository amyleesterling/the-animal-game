# Adult size evidence: 16 additional savanna animals

Researched 2026-09-21 for the field-notebook height scale. Sex-specific mass ranges below are adult figures where the source states that scope; pooled guide figures may not specify age. All units are metric. `—` means I did not find a defensible value, not zero. **Do not use length, shoulder height, or wingspan as ground-to-top-of-head standing height.** Sources rarely report a separate male and female standing-height *range*. A shared (sex-pooled) range must remain labelled as such in the interface. A reported "height" from a zoo is a useful broad species reference, but the source usually does not specify whether head feathers, casque, or horn tips are included. Species without a documented endpoint should be shown without a numerical head-height scale until a measured specimen or validated physically scaled 3D model is available; an image alone cannot supply that dimension.

## Implementation implications

1. Preserve the source's measurement type in data (`standingHeight`, `shoulderHeight`, `bodyLength`, `totalLength`, `mass`, `meanMass`) and sex scope (`male`, `female`, `pooled`). Do not convert one type to another from image pixels.
2. A sex-pooled standing-height range can be presented as a broad adult range for secretarybird, southern ground hornbill, helmeted guineafowl, grey crowned crane and white-backed vulture. The marabou source supplies only an upper bound. None of these sources supports **separate** male and female standing-height ranges.
3. For meerkat, oxpecker, roller, Nile monitor and other posture-sensitive species, let the student record an observation or compare with a known object, but leave the authoritative numerical head-top scale unavailable until a matched side-on measurement is documented.
4. For adult sex-specific mass, the useful range data here are ground hornbill, aardvark, meerkat, vervet monkey, and African buffalo. Olive baboon has sex-specific *means* rather than ranges. The rest need better data; don't copy a pooled range into separate male/female fields.

Heights mean **feet on level ground to the top of the resting head** as far as a source's “height” wording permits; bird sources generally do not publish a measurement protocol, so confirm the head/casque/crown endpoint before precise bar calibration. `Not established` is intentional. The three height facts already in the bird profiles—secretarybird, southern ground hornbill, and grey crowned crane—remain represented here. Weight is live body mass, not an image-derived estimate. ## Length audit and standing-height recheck (2026-09-21)

For this pass, **total length means bill/nose tip to tail tip, including the tail**. The [East African mammal field guide](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9781775840947_A21682936/preview-9781775840947_A21682936.pdf) explicitly uses that convention. Bird profiles usually say only “length”; the conventional bill-to-tail interpretation is plausible, but is not an individually documented measurement protocol. A length or shoulder-height figure must never fill the notebook's ground-to-top-of-head standing-height field. `—` means no defensible adult species range was found. A single value or a two-specimen span is labelled as such, rather than presented as a population range. Zoo “height” candidates remain approximate because their endpoints and stance are not specified.

**Import rule:** The five zoo/manual bird height ranges above, plus marabou's approximate standing range, are **provisional** height-bar references, requiring pose and crown/casque checks. No mammal, small bird, or Nile monitor has a validated adult ground-to-head range here. The length column supports size context, never vertical calibration. Keep two-specimen observations and mixed-species baboon figures in notes, not numeric species-range fields.

**Banded mongoose total-length follow-up.** [Animal Diversity Web's *Mungos mungo* account](https://animaldiversity.org/accounts/Mungos_mungo/) explicitly states that adults reach **0.55–0.60 m total body length** and discusses tail length separately. The consolidated [32-species table](ANIMAL_ADULT_SIZE_TABLE.md) uses that direct adult range rather than the two-specimen field-paper values noted above. Ground-to-head standing height remains unestablished.
